Why this webinar matters

What If Life Doesn't Go According to Plan?

An unexpected illness, a chronic condition, or the need for ongoing care can affect more than health. It can touch family finances, retirement savings, and the people who love and support you.

That's why it helps to understand your planning options before a crisis. This free session is educational, calm, and pressure-free, with no promises about financial outcomes.

What you'll learn

Four Ways to Get Prepared

🏥

Understanding Long-Term Care

What long-term care is, who may need it, and how the cost of care can affect personal finances and families.

🛡️

Understanding Living Benefits

How certain qualifying life insurance policies may allow access to a portion of the death benefit during the insured's lifetime, if policy conditions are met.

💰

Protecting Retirement Savings

How unexpected care expenses may affect retirement income, savings, and long-term financial goals.

👨‍👩‍👧

Planning for Your Family

Questions to consider, conversations to have with loved ones, and planning options to explore together.
